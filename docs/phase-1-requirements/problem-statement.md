# Problem Statement

## 1. Background
In conventional educational administration, student records, enrollment lists, attendance sheets, and grade records are often managed through decentralized spreadsheets, physical registers, or fragmented ad-hoc tools. 

---

## 2. Identified Core Problems

1. **Time-Consuming Manual Data Entry**:
   Administrators and faculty spend substantial time manually recording and compiling student profiles, course registrations, daily attendance, and exam scores.

2. **Data Duplication & Record Inaccuracies**:
   Decentralized data storage across departments leads to duplicate records, conflicting versions of student profiles, and transcription errors.

3. **Difficult Information Updates**:
   Modifying course details, assigning faculty, or updating student contact details across multiple separate documents is error-prone and lacks change traceability.

4. **Lack of Centralized Access**:
   Students, faculty, and administrative staff do not have a unified portal to access real-time status on enrollment, attendance percentage, or academic marks.

5. **Increased Administrative Burden**:
   Manual aggregation of attendance summaries and grade reports consumes valuable institutional time during semester examinations and accreditation reviews.

6. **Security & Role-Based Access Deficiencies**:
   Unsecured spreadsheets and manual logs lack fine-grained access control, creating risks of unauthorized tampering, identity exposure, and data loss.

---

## 3. Proposed Solution
The proposed solution is a centralized, secure, scalable, and maintainable **Student Information System (SIS)** developed through formal Object-Oriented Analysis and Design (OOAD). The system provides a unified platform with strict role-based access control, accurate data persistence, and clear modular separation for administrators, faculty, and students.\n
