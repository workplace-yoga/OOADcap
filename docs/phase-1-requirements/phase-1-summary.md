## 3. Summary of Frozen Scope
- **Actors (3)**: Admin, Faculty, Student.
- **Core Modules (6)**:
  1. Authentication & Role Management
  2. Student Management
  3. Faculty Management
  4. Course & Enrollment Management
  5. Attendance Management
  6. Academic Records & Marks Management
- **Key Candidate Domain Entities**: `User`, `Admin`, `Faculty`, `Student`, `Course`, `Enrollment`, `Attendance`, `AcademicRecord`.

## 4. Next Step: Transition to Phase 2
With Phase 1 requirements formally approved and frozen, the project is ready to advance to **Phase 2 — Object-Oriented Analysis (OOA)**, which will involve:
1. Identifying formal Use Cases and writing detailed Use Case specifications.
2. Creating Use Case Diagrams.
3. Formulating System Sequence Diagrams (SSDs).
4. Refining the Domain Model and identifying conceptual classes, attributes, and associations.
5. Defining object responsibilities and CRC (Class-Responsibility-Collaborator) cards.\n
